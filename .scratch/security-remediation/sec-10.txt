## What to build

Two changes:
1. **Password complexity validation**: Require 1 uppercase, 1 lowercase, 1 digit, 1 special character, min 8 chars. Apply server-side in register, admin/users.php, and admin/change-password.php.
2. **Generic auth error messages in login.php**: Return identical "Invalid email or password." for unverified accounts, wrong password, and non-existent users. Add timing normalization (random sleep 0-100ms) to normalize response times.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Passwords without uppercase are rejected server-side with clear message
- [ ] Passwords without lowercase are rejected
- [ ] Passwords without digit are rejected
- [ ] Passwords without special character are rejected
- [ ] Passwords under 8 chars are rejected
- [ ] Login returns identical message for: non-existent email, wrong password, unverified account
- [ ] Response time variation within expected range (timing attack mitigated)
- [ ] Client-side strength meter still works in parallel
