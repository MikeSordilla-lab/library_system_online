# Product

product

## Users

LibraLink serves public library guests, borrowers, librarians, and admins. Guests browse the catalog and decide whether to register. Borrowers sign in to manage loans, due dates, reservations, reading history, and account details. Librarians and admins sign in to manage circulation, inventory, users, reservations, receipts, and audit trails. Users are often task-focused and may be using shared or public devices, so authentication screens must be clear, reassuring, and secure.

## Product Purpose

LibraLink is a web-based library management system that streamlines catalog discovery, borrower self-service, staff circulation workflows, reservation management, and receipt generation. Success means patrons can quickly access their account and staff can perform library operations accurately, with secure role-based access and dependable audit logging.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, scholarly. The product should feel like a modern public-library portal: grounded, readable, welcoming, and quietly protective of user data.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, flashy fintech-style security pages, hostile lockout copy, decorative error screens that hide the next step, and designs that feel playful at the expense of trust. Avoid making authentication failures feel like a dead end.

## Design Principles

- Security should feel protective, not punitive.
- Give every blocked state a clear next action and a human reason.
- Preserve library warmth while keeping task flows direct and fast.
- Make role-based workflows obvious without visual clutter.
- Prioritize readable, accessible interfaces over ornament.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Authentication and error states must be keyboard-accessible, screen-reader clear, and readable at mobile sizes. Respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain strong contrast, and avoid relying on color alone to communicate status.
