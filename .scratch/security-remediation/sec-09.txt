## What to build

Two changes:
1. **Session cookie flags in `bootstrap.php`**: Add `session_set_cookie_params(['httponly' => true, 'secure' => true, 'samesite' => 'Strict'])`
2. **Session regeneration on privilege changes**: Add `session_regenerate_id(true)` after password change, role change, and logout

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `session_set_cookie_params` sets `httponly`, `secure`, and `samesite` flags
- [ ] Existing sessions continue to work after cookie param change
- [ ] `session_regenerate_id(true)` called after successful password change
- [ ] `session_regenerate_id(true)` called after role change (admin/librarian actions)
- [ ] `session_regenerate_id(true)` called on logout
- [ ] Cookie inspect shows `HttpOnly`, `Secure`, `SameSite=Strict` flags
