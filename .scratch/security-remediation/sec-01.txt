## What to build

Implement an in-memory sliding-window rate limiter as a new `src/utils/rate_limit.php` module with functions `rate_limit_check($action, $max_attempts, $window_seconds)` and `rate_limit_headers()`. Apply it to login, register, verify (OTP), and forgot-password endpoints. OTP verify should lock after 5 wrong attempts for 15 minutes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/utils/rate_limit.php` exists with `rate_limit_check()` and `rate_limit_headers()`
- [ ] Login endpoint is rate limited (e.g. 5 attempts per 15 minutes)
- [ ] Register endpoint is rate limited
- [ ] OTP verify endpoint locks after 5 wrong attempts for 15 minutes
- [ ] Forgot-password endpoint is rate limited
- [ ] Rate limit headers (`X-RateLimit-Limit`, `X-RateLimit-Remaining`, `X-RateLimit-Reset`) are set on responses
- [ ] Existing tests pass; new unit tests cover rate limit logic
